Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Welcome to PacketSence. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use PacketSence, an application designed to help users explore packaged-food ingredients, nutrition facts, declared allergens, additives, and other available product information.

Our approach: PacketSence is designed to provide product information with minimal data handling. Certain features and third-party services may process information as described below.

1. Information We Process

Product searches and barcodes

When you search for a product or scan its barcode, the relevant search query or barcode may be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve matching product information.

Camera permission

The app may request camera access to scan product barcodes. Camera access is used for barcode scanning. The app is designed not to upload camera images or video to our own servers. You can manage camera permission through your device settings. Manual search or barcode entry may be available without camera access.

Local app data

PacketSence may store recent scans and lightweight preferences locally on your device. This supports features such as scan history and a convenient user experience. The exact data stored depends on the app version and features you use.

2. How We Use Information

  • To retrieve packaged-food product information.
  • To provide barcode scanning and product search.
  • To display available ingredients, nutrition facts, declared allergens, additives, and food classifications.
  • To maintain local scan history and preferences where supported.
  • To support advertising, app diagnostics, and reliability where applicable.

3. Third-Party Services

PacketSence may rely on third-party services to provide product information and other app features. These services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Open Food Facts

Product lookup requests may be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve available packaged-food information.

Privacy policy: Open Food Facts Privacy Policy

Google AdMob

If advertising is enabled in your version of PacketSence, Google AdMob may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic data, subject to the advertising configuration, applicable consent, device settings, and Google's policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Data Sharing

PacketSence's initial version is designed to operate without a custom application backend or mandatory account registration. However, product lookups and enabled third-party SDKs may transmit relevant information directly to their service providers.

We do not sell users' personal information. Third-party providers may process information according to their own policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information may be deleted using available in-app controls, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app, subject to your device's behavior and the app's implementation.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to their retention and deletion practices. PacketSence does not control data retained by those services.

6. Children's Privacy

PacketSence is a general-audience application and is not specifically directed at children under 13. The current version does not require account registration. Third-party services, including advertising providers if enabled, may have additional age-related requirements.

If you have a concern about privacy or information handling, please contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit information handled by the app and protect locally stored app information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Food Information Disclaimer

Product information may be incomplete, outdated, unavailable, or inaccurate. Always check the actual packaging for ingredients, allergen declarations, nutrition values, and other important details.

PacketSence provides general informational content and is not a substitute for professional medical or dietary advice. It does not guarantee that a product is suitable or safe for any particular person.

9. Your Choices

You can manage camera permission through your device settings and remove locally stored app data using available app or device controls. Additional choices relating to advertising and other third-party services may be available depending on your location and the services enabled.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, services, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page along with a revised effective date when appropriate.
